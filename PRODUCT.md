# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Two primary audiences, each served by its own landing page. Emerging brands: founders and operators with real capability but a story that hasn't caught up, evaluating whether Throughline Co. can find and sharpen their narrative. Established brands: leaders at mature companies whose business has outgrown its market perception, evaluating whether Throughline Co. can close that gap without alienating existing customers. Both arrive skeptical of agency theater and are won over by demonstrated, in-progress proof rather than polished case-study retrospectives.

## Product Purpose

Throughline Co. is a purpose-driven brand strategy studio. The site's job is to convert visitors into discovery calls by proving, through live and ongoing engagements, that the studio does real positioning work rather than surface-level rebrands. Success is a booked discovery call, informed by genuine belief that Throughline understands the visitor's specific situation.

## Positioning

The gap between what a business is and what the market believes it is, is a story problem, not a logo problem. Throughline Co. closes that gap through positioning and narrative work, documented transparently as it happens rather than shown only after the fact.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Book a discovery call
- Secondary: soft CTAs embedded mid-page ("Want this for your brand? Book a call.")
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: this is a story problem, not a logo problem
- Belief ladder: recognize the gap (past perception vs. current capability vs. new market belief) → see it made concrete via an interactive diagnostic → see it solved in real time via live, dated case-study trackers for real clients → trust the studio enough to book a call

## Brand Personality

Editorial, warm, plainspoken, quietly confident. Voice tells clients the truth even when it complicates the pitch (e.g. surfacing a false choice a client was stuck on) rather than flattering them into a sale. Calm rather than hype-driven; proof over promises. No em dashes anywhere on the site, commas or full stops instead.

## Anti-references

Not generic SaaS marketing (hero-metric templates, gradient text, stock "team high-fiving" photography). Not agency-brag case studies that only show the polished after; the site's differentiator is showing the messy during, dated and updated in public.

## Design Principles

- Show the during, not just the after — live-updating trackers over static case studies
- Honesty over flattery — the copy names the client's actual stuck point before pitching the fix
- One consistent brand system across every page and every client-facing tool, no per-page reinvention
- Proof is specific and dated, never vague ("shipped July 17, 2026" not "recently launched")
- Interactive tools take real input and return one honest, specific paragraph, not a scored quiz or gamified output

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Standard reduced-motion support already present in the codebase pattern (scroll-reveal, cursor dot, hover states) should extend to new interactive components.
