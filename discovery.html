Section 01 of 03

Your brand

Who you are and what you stand for.

Q 01
If your brand disappeared tomorrow, what would your clients actually miss?
Not the service. The specific thing only you do that they can't get anywhere else.
Q 02
What do you believe about your industry that most people won't say out loud?
The thing that makes you roll your eyes at how it's usually done.
Q 03
What would you never do as a brand, even if it made you more money?
Your non-negotiables define your brand as much as your values do.
Section 02 of 03

Your client

The person you're building this for.

Q 04
Describe the client you'd clone if you could. What made them the perfect fit?
A real person if you can picture one. Where were they in their business? What did they care about?
Q 05
What is your client's real problem — the one underneath the one they tell you about?
They come to you saying one thing. What's actually keeping them up at night?
Q 06
Who is the wrong client for you — and why?
Be honest. This protects your time and theirs.
Section 03 of 03

Your proof

The results and the thing only you do.

Q 07
What's the best result you've ever created for a client? What specifically changed for them?
Numbers if you have them. Story if you don't. Both is best.
Q 08
What is the one thing about how you work that no one else does — that you've never quite figured out how to put on a website?
A process, a question you ask, a way you see things. The thing clients mention that you didn't expect.
