The real problem

Established companies in mature industries face disruptive change. The business restructures, grows, and evolves, but the brand stays stuck in the past. Consistent messaging over time is what builds familiarity in new markets, and established brands struggle here because their perception hasn't kept pace with what they can actually do.

The gap isn't the logo. It's the space between what you were, what you are now, and what new markets still think you are. Most established brands respond with a visual refresh when what they actually need is a positioning shift.
