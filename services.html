What we actually do

What we actually do.

Brand Strategy

Positioning, voice, message hierarchy. We find the thread that connects your mission to your audience and make sure every touchpoint pulls on it. You leave knowing exactly what to say, who to say it to, and why it lands. Deliverables: a positioning statement, a message hierarchy, and a voice guide your whole team can actually use — not a deck that sits in a drive folder.

Audience Growth

Not follower counts. Real people who care about what you do and come back. We build the kind of presence that earns attention over time, on the platforms that actually matter for your audience. That means channel selection, a realistic content cadence, and community tactics built around your actual audience, not a generic growth playbook.

Storytelling and Content

You have a story worth telling. Most organizations do. The problem is it usually lives in the founder's head, or in a grant report nobody reads. We dig it out, make it sharp, and turn it into content that moves people. Deliverables: a core narrative framework, case-study copy, and a content library you can keep pulling from long after we hand it off.

LinkedIn Strategy

For founders and organizations where LinkedIn is the primary growth channel. We build a presence that generates real conversations, not just impressions — a posting cadence, content pillars, and a founder-voice framework built around what your specific audience actually engages with, not what's trending that week.

Online and Offline Marketing

Social campaigns, donor communications, conference presence, community events. We build strategies that work in the room and outside it — from a donor email to a booth banner, everything ties back to the same throughline instead of feeling like six disconnected efforts.

Website Design & Build

From clean static sites to fully animated, tool-integrated builds. We design and build the thing itself, then make sure it is actually doing its job — converting the right people, not just looking good. Static builds ship fast; motion and tool integrations like booking, forms, and CRM connections get layered in as scope grows.

The process

What working together actually looks like.

01

We start with your audience, not your org.

Before we write a single word, we build a real picture of who you are trying to reach. Not a demographic. A person. What they are afraid of. What they are searching for. What would make them stop and read. That is what drives everything else.

02

We find the story you have been sitting on.

Every organization has one. The founding moment nobody knows about. The client whose life changed because of the work. The reason this exists that never makes it into the marketing. We find it. That becomes your throughline.

03

We build the infrastructure.

Messaging, voice, content, platform presence. Not a strategy deck that sits in a folder. Real, working assets your team can use this week.

04

We measure whether it is working.

Audience growth, engagement, conversions. We watch the numbers and adjust when something is not landing. Data is not a quarterly check-in. It is how we know we are building the right thing.

05

We make sure you do not need us forever.

We train the person inside your organization who will own this when we leave. That is not a disclaimer. That is the goal. Most clients get there within six months. That is how you know the strategy actually works.

Why impact marketing is different

Anyone can run ads. Not everyone can do this.

Product marketing is about features, comparisons, price points. Impact marketing is about trust.

Your audience — whether that is donors, community members, or customers who buy because they believe in what you stand for — they do not convert on a clever headline. They convert when they feel something real. When they see themselves in your story. When they believe you are actually doing what you say you are doing.

That takes more than good copy. It takes community building, persona-level research, and emotional storytelling that is backed by data on what is actually working. It takes someone who has done this for mission-driven organizations specifically, not someone who will try a product playbook and wonder why the numbers are not moving.
